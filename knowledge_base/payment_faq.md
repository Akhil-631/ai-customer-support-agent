# Payment FAQ

## Accepted Payment Methods

Available payment methods may depend on the customer's location, order, and checkout configuration.

Customers should use the payment methods displayed during checkout.

## Payment Failure

A payment may fail because of incorrect payment information, insufficient funds, payment-provider issues, bank restrictions, or temporary technical problems.

Customers may retry the payment using an available payment method.

## Duplicate Payment

A customer may occasionally observe what appears to be a duplicate payment or duplicate charge.

This can sometimes occur because a payment authorization and the final payment appear separately in the customer's account.

If the customer believes that they have actually been charged twice, the case should be reviewed by customer support.

## Missing Payment

If a customer believes that a payment was made but the order does not reflect the payment, additional investigation may be required.

The customer should provide the relevant Order ID and payment information where available.

## Payment Status

The payment status associated with a specific order should be verified using the appropriate business system when such information is available.

General payment questions can be answered using this FAQ.

## Payment Disputes

Cases involving suspected unauthorized charges, repeated deductions, disputed transactions, or other payment-related complaints may require human support.

## Important Note

This document provides general payment information.

It should not be used to determine the actual payment status of a customer's specific transaction when that information must be retrieved from a business system.
