# Return Policy

## Return Eligibility

Customers may request a return for eligible products subject to the applicable return conditions.

The product may need to be in an acceptable condition and may need to be returned within the applicable return period.

## Return Window

Eligible products should generally be returned within the return period specified for the order or product category.

The applicable return period may vary depending on the product.

## Product Condition

Returned products may be required to be unused or in an acceptable condition.

Original packaging, accessories, documentation, or other included items may be required depending on the product.

## Non-Returnable Products

Certain products or product categories may not be eligible for return because of their nature, condition, or applicable restrictions.

Customers should confirm eligibility before sending an item back.

## Return Process

Customers requesting a return should provide their Order ID and relevant product information.

Customer support may review the order and determine whether the product qualifies for return.

## Return Shipping

Return shipping requirements may depend on the reason for the return and the applicable return conditions.

Customers should follow the return instructions provided by customer support.

## Refund After Return

A refund may be processed after the returned product has been received and the return has been reviewed.

The timing of the refund may depend on the applicable refund policy and payment provider.

## Important Note

This document describes general return policy information.

For a specific order, the support agent should verify the customer's order information using the appropriate business system.
