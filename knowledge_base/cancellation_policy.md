# Cancellation Policy

## Order Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation of an order before the order reaches a stage where cancellation is no longer possible.

Cancellation availability depends on the current status of the order.

## Cancellation Before Processing

Orders that have not entered processing may generally be eligible for cancellation.

The support system should verify the current order status before attempting cancellation.

## Orders Already Shipped

An order that has already been shipped may not be cancellable through the standard cancellation process.

In such cases, the customer may need to follow the applicable return process after delivery.

## Cancellation Request

Customers requesting cancellation should provide their Order ID.

The support agent should verify the order before attempting to cancel it.

## Cancellation Result

A cancellation request does not automatically mean that an order has been cancelled.

The business system must confirm whether the cancellation was successful.

## Refund After Cancellation

If payment has already been captured and the order is successfully cancelled, an applicable refund may be initiated according to the refund policy.

The time required for the refund to appear depends on the payment provider.

## Important Note

General cancellation rules should be retrieved from this policy.

Actual cancellation of a specific order must be performed or verified using the appropriate business tool.
