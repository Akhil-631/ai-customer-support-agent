# Refund Policy

## Refund Eligibility

Customers may request a refund for eligible orders when the product or service meets the applicable refund conditions.

Refund eligibility may depend on the product category, order status, cancellation status, and whether the order has already been delivered.

A refund cannot be guaranteed solely because a customer requests one. The applicable order and product conditions must be reviewed.

## Refund Processing

Once a refund has been approved, the refund is submitted for processing through the original payment method whenever possible.

The time required for the refund to appear in the customer's account may depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

## Refund Timeline

Approved refunds are generally expected to be credited within 5–7 business days after the refund has been processed.

Some payment providers may require additional processing time.

## Refund Status

Customers asking about an existing refund should provide their Order ID whenever possible.

The support system can check the status of an existing refund associated with an order.

## Refund Delays

If the expected refund period has passed and the customer has not received the refund, the case may require additional investigation by customer support.

Customers should provide the relevant Order ID and any available payment information when contacting support.

## Refund Amount

The refund amount depends on the amount eligible for reimbursement under the applicable order and refund conditions.

Additional charges, shipping charges, discounts, or other adjustments may affect the final refund amount.

## Important Note

This document describes general refund policy information.

For the status of a specific customer's refund, the support agent should use the appropriate business tool rather than relying only on this policy.
