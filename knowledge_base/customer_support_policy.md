# Customer Support Policy

## When Human Support Is Required

Some customer issues cannot be resolved safely through automated database lookups or general knowledge.

These cases should be escalated to human customer support.

## Payment Issues

Issues involving suspected duplicate charges, disputed payments, unauthorized transactions, or other payment complaints may require human investigation.

## Complaints

Customer complaints that require investigation, judgment, or resolution beyond standard automated workflows should be escalated.

## Damaged Products

Reports of damaged products may require human review because the support team may need additional information, photographs, or other evidence.

## Missing Products

Reports that a product is missing from an order may require investigation by customer support.

## Technical Issues

Technical problems that cannot be resolved through the available automated support tools should be escalated.

## Legal Issues

Requests involving legal matters should be escalated to an appropriate human support representative.

## Customer Request for an Agent

If a customer explicitly asks to speak with a human support representative, the request should be escalated.

## Low Confidence

If the AI system has insufficient confidence in its classification or cannot determine an appropriate action, the request should be reviewed by human support.

## Information Customers May Provide

Customers may be asked for information such as:

- Order ID
- Relevant payment information
- Description of the issue
- Dates associated with the transaction
- Additional supporting information

## Important Note

Human escalation does not mean that every request requires manual processing.

Requests that can be safely resolved using verified business data or approved knowledge may be handled automatically.
